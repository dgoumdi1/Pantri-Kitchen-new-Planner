- Taking a picture using the phone camera.
- Uploading an existing image.
- Scanning a grocery receipt.
- Photographing a fridge, pantry, or individual food items.

For receipts:
- Extract food products and quantities when visible.
- Ignore totals, payment information, taxes, discounts, and non-food purchases.
- Turn abbreviated product names into readable names.
- Preserve the original detected text for review.

For fridge, pantry, and item photos:
- Identify visible food items.
- Suggest pantry, fridge, or freezer storage.
- Estimate counts only when reasonably supported by the image.

- Mark uncertain results as “Needs review.”

- Change quantity and unit.
- Choose storage location.
- Enter an expiration date or leave it unknown.
- Remove incorrect detections.
- Select which items to import.
- Confirm “Add selected items.”

Detect potential duplicates and let users choose whether to merge quantities or create a separate item. Do not merge incompatible units automatically. Prevent duplicate submissions from adding items twice.

Include camera permission handling, image previews, upload limits, file validation, progress indicators, retry options, and manual entry as a fallback.

Avoid retaining uploaded receipt or food images unnecessarily. Explain processing and retention clearly to the user.
